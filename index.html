Ten Grand: Life Simulator: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Ten Grand: Life Simulator ("the game") is made by Amir Asaraf. This page explains what the game does with your data.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress on your device: your current life, your past lives, your settings, and a local log of the moves you make in the game. This data stays on your device. The game has no account and no login, and it doesn't ask for your name, email or age.

Play stats

To learn whether the game is fun and to fix crashes, the game sends an anonymous copy of its play log to our server after a session, when your device is online. It contains:

  • How you play: the screens you see, the cards you're offered and pick, their outcomes, when a session starts and ends, and the time zone offset (so "the next day" means your next day).
  • Crashes: the type of error and where in the game's code it happened.
  • A random ID the game makes when it's first opened (shown in Settings → About), and the game's build number.

It doesn't contain your name, email, contacts, location, photos, your device's advertising ID or anything else about you. The data is sent encrypted (HTTPS) and stored on our own server, hosted by Cloudflare, which processes it only on our behalf. We use it only to analyse and improve the game. We don't sell it, share it with anyone else or use it for advertising.

Play stats are optional: turn off Settings → Share play stats and the game sends nothing more.

Sharing data yourself

Two features in Settings let you share data yourself:

  • Export saves your lives and play log to a file in a place you choose.
  • Send a report opens your phone's share menu with a text that contains the game's build number, the content version and your last 20 moves. You choose the app and the recipient. If you send it to us, we use it only to fix problems.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase everything, or uninstalling the game, deletes everything stored on your device. Play stats already sent are kept, with no name on them, for as long as we use them to improve the game. To have them deleted, email us the ID from Settings → About and we'll delete everything sent with that ID.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Future versions may add features such as advertising or in-app purchases, which may involve third-party services. If that happens, this policy will be updated before those versions are released, and the date at the top will change.
